Thank you for installing mathisfun.

To see how to access the service:

1. Get the service name:
   kubectl get svc -l app.kubernetes.io/name={{ include "mathisfun.name" . }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

2. If you installed with `helm install`, run:
   kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "mathisfun.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

Then open http://localhost:8080
